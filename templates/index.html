HOW TO PLAY SPIT!

🎯 Goal: Be the first to empty all 26 cards (your 5 hand cards and your 21 draw stock cards)!


⚡ Fast-Paced Card Placement (+/- 1 Rank):

There are no turns! Play as fast as you can. Play any card from your 5-card hand that is 1 above or 1 below either central pile.

Ace wraps with King and 2! (e.g. King on Ace, Ace on King, 2 on Ace, Ace on 2).


🔄 Simultaneous Spit Flipping:

If neither player has a valid move, tap SPIT! (or press Space). Cards will ONLY flip when BOTH players have called SPIT! If your opponent or SpitBot calls Spit first, your Spit button will flash—tap it to flip together!


⌨️ Controls & Shortcuts:

  • Click & Play: Click a card in your hand, then click Pile 1 or Pile 2.
  • Drag & Drop: Drag any hand card directly onto Pile 1 or 2.
  • Keys 1, 2, 3, 4, 5: Select card 1 through 5 in hand.
  • Key Q: Play selected card to Pile 1.
  • Key W or E: Play selected card to Pile 2.
  • Space or S: Tap SPIT!
  • Enter: Request Rematch on game over.
